Margin Fight

Blue and Red, two ballpoint stick figures, fight across a notebook page. The fight is choreographed live, so no two rounds play out the same.

Each figure is eleven joint angles. A move is a short list of keyframed poses, eased from one to the next, with the lowest point of the body pinned to the ground line. Hits trigger a few frames of hit-stop, ink spray and screen shake.
