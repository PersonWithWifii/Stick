Margin Fight

Blue and Red, two ballpoint stick figures, fight across a notebook page. Power-ups and weapons fall onto the page, and the fighters race to grab them.

Orbs grant a random anime power: Kamehameha (with beam struggles), Domain Expansion, Za Warudo, Rasengan, Shadow Clone Jutsu, Super Saiyan, Gum-Gum Pistol, Spirit Bomb, Instant Transmission, Black Flash and Getsuga Tensho. Weapons are a katana, a bo staff, a war hammer, a No. 2 pencil and shuriken, and each one breaks after a few hits. Every figure is thirteen joint angles, keyframed and eased, with the lowest point pinned to the ground line.
